# Workflow completion v1 — cold decisions and deferred gaps

## Deliberate non-decisions

- No seventh phase or owner change.
- No production deployment, secret use, CI policy mutation, commit, push, PR, merge, release, or npm publication.
- No authentication/container/network/isolation architecture chosen for the Web runner.
- No risk-adaptive rewrite of Architecture evidence floors.
- No replacement of the seven engineering artifact IDs.
- No implicit migration of project-owned initialized prompts/templates.

## Why these remain open

They require a human architecture, security, scope, or release decision under the repository escalation gates. The V1 records the defect/risk, adds fail-closed local validation where already implied by the current model, and avoids claiming a stronger guarantee than the implementation provides.
